# Value Proposition

Bento is redefining yield generation by offering stable and convenient risk-adjusted yields on mainstream digital assets in a decentralized way, addressing both user needs and the current challenges in the DeFi space.&#x20;

**Unified & Optimized Yields**\
X-Bento Savings Rate aggregates and optimises yield from X-denominated DeFi, CeFi and TradFi sources by dynamically allocating across diverse set of collateral assets and yield generating strategies.

**Unified & Omni-chain Liquidity**\
bentoX unifies the liquidity of growing number of stablecoins, ETH, and BTC derivatives into a single token. Both bentoX and bentoX+ are ERC-20 tokens built on the LayerZero OFT Standard, allowing seamless transfer of assets and prices across multiple blockchains, ensuring efficient and unified liquidity management.

**Unified UX**\
bentoX assets eliminate the need for active management and remove the unnecessary complexities and inefficiencies of dealing with underlying infrastructure and yield strategies like multiple assets and platforms, no auto-compounding, protocol upgrades, gas costs etc.&#x20;

**Diversified Collateral**\
bentoX assets are backed by a diverse range of collateral assets allocated across multiple yield generating strategies, each utilizing different mechanism designs and offering different yield along with carrying its own set of risks. The bentoX reserve design effectively isolates the risks from each other and minimises exposure to the risks associated with any single mechanism or strategy.

**Decentralized Asset Management**\
The protocol operates under a DAO-based governance model, with both bentoX and BENTO token holders participating in decision-making. The DAO manages the underlying collateral and protocols through a transparent on-chain voting process.

**Dual Rewards Model**\
Users can choose between stable, organic yields with guaranteed returns or boosted returns that offer upside potential from governance tokens. This unique economic model is designed to distribute value and governance power to the long-term users and reward early participation.

### Bento: The Future of DeFi Savings

By pioneering DeFi-native savings products, Bento is driving mass adoption and positioning itself as the fintech of the future—just as Aave and MakerDAO have redefined the future of decentralized banking infrastructure.
