# Governance

Bento is a decentralized protocol governed by a DAO that includes all holders of locked bentoX (bentoX++) and locked BENTO governance tokens. Token holders possess voting rights to propose and implement changes to the protocol, oversee the protocol's treasury, and enable on-chain asset management by whitelisting the underlying collateral assets and protocols through a transparent on-chain voting process.
