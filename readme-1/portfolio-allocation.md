# Portfolio Allocation

In the bentoX ecosystem, each collateral asset is assigned a weight that reflects its proportion within the reserve backing the bentoX token. Collateral assets are then further utlised for generating yield on multiple DeFi protocols offering diverse yield strategies with varies risk profiles. These weights are dynamically adjusted to keep the reserves diversified and balanced, ensuring an optimal risk-adjusted yield for users. In the launch phase rebalancing will be based on rule-based strategies potentially building specialized AI agents for yield optimization. After initial launch, Bento will follow a modular approach and offer it’s platform to whitelisted instituiitons to curate their own multi-asset multi-stratgey yield generating synthetics.

Bento is at the forefront of decentralised on-chain asset management, providing automated, optimised yields. The protocol operates under a DAO-based governance model, where both bentoX and BENTO token holders actively participate in managing the underlying collateral assets and protocols through transparent on-chain voting.

Bento prioritizes safety and stability, adopting top-tier security practices and selecting high-quality collateral assets and DeFi platforms. New collateral assets and yield-generating protocols are reviewed by DAO-appointed risk managers under a robust risk framework and require DAO approval. Each asset has predefined minimum and maximum weight limits, as well as caps on exposure to individual protocols, reducing the risk of over-dependence on any single asset or protocol in case of de-pegging or exploits.\
